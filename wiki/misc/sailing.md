# Sailing Notes
### Hoisting a Kite
- Bow and mast get sail bag set on leward rail, pole is attatched to nub and hoisted up so it is parallel with deck and
alongside jib with working guy attatched to outboard end.
- Bow calls "sneak" and the working guy is taken in enough for tack to come into contact withpole, once this happens
bow calls "made" and kite is ready to hoist.
- Kite halyard is hoisted quickly as possible to ensure proper filling of the kite.
- Once kite is up headsail is furled.

### Gybing a Kite
- Helm calls "ready to gybe" and bears away onto a run; mast stands ready to trip pole, bow to guide pole.
- Helm calls "trip"; mast trips pole and raises inboard end to allow bow to swing pole around forestay.
Once pole is attatched to new tack bow calls "made" and working guy is sneaked up. Inboard end is lowered.
- Helm calls "gybing" and swings the boat through the gybe as usual and kite is trimmed by pit.

### Dropping a kite
- Bow opens fore hatch and pole is eased to forestay.
- Jib is unfurled to shade kite and bow pulls the working guy around the headstay and detatches it from the sail.
- Bow calls "drop" and the halyard is lowered fast while bow and mast gather up the sail and throw it down the hatch.
- Mast secures pole and assists bow with re packing kite.
